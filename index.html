Cut to the run

Each patch cable is measured on your board and built to that exact length with Mogami cable. Nothing loops, pulls or sits loose.

Routed under the deck

Runs are tied down beneath the board, out from under your feet and easy to follow when something goes wrong.

Every connection checked

Hum, crackle and dropouts are traced to the cause, whether a worn cable, a loose jack or a bad power run, and the failing part is replaced.

Back to the whole board

A board that looks clean, is easy to troubleshoot, and works every time you step on stage.

Cleanup & troubleshooting

Crackles and dropouts usually mean a worn cable, a loose jack or a connection under strain. Every connection is checked and the failing part replaced.

Re-cable

Every cable on the board is replaced with one made to the length of its run, using Mogami cable, then installed and tied down. No slack coiled under the board, nothing pulling on a jack.

Bring the board with the pedals mounted where you want them.

Full rebuild

For a new pedal, a new signal order or a board that has outgrown its layout. The layout is planned with you, then everything goes back with new cabling.
